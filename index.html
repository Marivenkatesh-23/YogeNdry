The product

Smart sensors on the machines you already have

One small module per washer turns a legacy laundry room into a connected one.

Benefits

One system, two winners

Property managers protect their machines. Residents get their time back.

For Property Managers

Fewer breakdowns, longer-lasting machines

  • Predictive maintenance alerts

    Abnormal vibration flags worn bearings, loose drums and failing motors early, so you schedule repairs instead of reacting to breakdowns.

  • Machine lifespan extension

    Run-hour tracking per machine lets you spread the load evenly and service on real wear, not guesswork.

  • Drum overloading prevention

    Overloaded or unbalanced drums have a distinct vibration signature. Residents get an instant nudge to fix the load.

For Residents & Students

Know before you go downstairs

  • Real-time machine tracking

    See which washers are free, and when busy ones finish, from your room.

  • No more physical queues

    Get notified when a machine frees up instead of waiting beside it with a laundry bag.

  • Gamified textile donation

    Donate clothes you no longer wear and earn reward points in the app. See Give Back
